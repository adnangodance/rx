# ScriptRx landing-page system

## Conversion narrative

Build the page in this order unless the task requires otherwise:

1. Clear health outcome and primary action
2. Trust signals and simple qualifiers
3. Visual treatment or product pathways
4. Product catalogue with transparent pricing language
5. How care works
6. Clinical credibility
7. Questions and objections
8. Footer navigation and legal access

Do not force every page to include every section. Remove sections that repeat the same message.

## Hero

- Use a bright white canvas with a concise announcement bar only when there is a meaningful update.
- Keep navigation quiet and compact.
- Use a large, conversational headline with controlled line breaks.
- Place two to four compact trust points beside or beneath the headline.
- Follow with one or two large visual story cards and smaller treatment shortcuts.
- Contain color inside cards rather than tinting the whole page.
- Ensure product imagery does not cover headings or CTAs at any breakpoint.

Good headline qualities:

- Outcome-led
- Plain language
- Two short lines
- No guaranteed medical result

## Product catalogue

- Use a meaningful category title such as “Longevity & everyday health.”
- Display four cards on wide screens, two on tablets, and a horizontal snap row or one column on mobile.
- Separate product artwork from product information.
- Art panel: 340–380px desktop, warm-white surface, 18–24px radius.
- Information order: name, “From” price, category or active ingredient, primary action, details.
- Keep metadata tight; the product image is the visual focus.
- Product hover may float the render while its contact shadow tightens.
- Do not imply that adding to cart guarantees prescribing or eligibility.

## FAQ

- Use a white section.
- Place a compact two-line heading above the list.
- Use separate rounded warm-white rows with generous horizontal padding.
- Keep plus controls visually simple.
- Use native disclosure semantics or an accessible accordion.
- Open one or multiple items according to the existing product pattern; never require precise pointer interaction.

## Footer

- Use a near-black rounded panel on white.
- Keep link columns small and quiet.
- Include clear access to categories, company, main navigation, privacy, terms, and telehealth information.
- Use a large ScriptRx wordmark as the visual anchor.

## Copy

- Use “care,” “treatment options,” “licensed provider,” “if prescribed,” and “availability varies” precisely.
- Prefer “Start a visit,” “Explore options,” or “See treatments” over vague labels.
- Show real price units and recurring cadence.
- Keep sentences short and avoid hype.

## Responsive behavior

- Protect heading readability before preserving side-by-side layout.
- Turn multi-column hero story cards into a single priority card on narrow screens.
- Make product rows horizontally scrollable only when cards retain sufficient width and snap cleanly.
- Keep touch targets at least 44px.
- Test long product names, answers, pricing qualifiers, and browser text zoom.
