# UX and UI release checklist

## Purpose and hierarchy

- Can a new user explain the page purpose in five seconds?
- Is the primary action obvious?
- Does every section add new information?
- Are headings and labels concrete?

## Healthcare trust

- Are claims supportable and appropriately qualified?
- Is provider review clear where treatment eligibility is involved?
- Are price, cadence, insurance, shipping, and availability transparent?
- Are privacy and consent links available at the right moment?
- Are placeholders used instead of invented clinical facts?

## Interaction

- Do all controls work with keyboard and touch?
- Are hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, loading, empty, success, and error states covered?
- Does navigation preserve useful context?
- Are destructive or consequential actions confirmed?
- Is recovery possible after errors?

## Accessibility

- Is semantic HTML used?
- Does every control have an accessible name?
- Is focus visible?
- Is contrast sufficient?
- Is status communicated beyond color?
- Do animations respect reduced motion?
- Are touch targets at least 44px?

## Responsive quality

- Is there no accidental horizontal overflow?
- Do headings wrap intentionally?
- Do product images remain contained?
- Are tables transformed according to information priority?
- Are mobile actions reachable and unobscured?

## Visual polish

- Is spacing based on a consistent rhythm?
- Are radii and shadows consistent by component type?
- Are product images sharp and correctly scaled?
- Are divider, muted text, and surface colors restrained?
- Is motion subtle and purposeful?

## Conversion

- Does each page offer a clear next step?
- Are objections answered near the decision point?
- Is the CTA specific and honest?
- Is important qualifying information visible before commitment?
